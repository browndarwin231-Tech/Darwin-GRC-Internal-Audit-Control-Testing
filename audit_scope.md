# Internal Audit Scope

## Audit Title

Internal Security Controls & Compliance Review

## Organization

Northbridge Technologies

## Audit Type

Simulated Internal Audit

## Audit Objective

The objective of this audit is to evaluate whether selected security and compliance controls are properly designed, implemented, documented, and operating effectively.

The audit also assesses whether identified weaknesses are appropriately documented, assigned a risk rating, and tracked through remediation.

---

## Audit Scope

The review includes the following control areas:

- User provisioning
- User termination
- Privileged access
- Multi-factor authentication
- Access reviews
- Change management
- Audit logging
- Vulnerability management
- Incident response
- Backup and recovery
- Third-party risk management

---

## Systems in Scope

The simulated environment includes:

- Microsoft Entra ID
- Microsoft 365
- Cloud infrastructure
- Windows endpoints
- Centralized SIEM
- Vulnerability scanner
- Ticketing platform
- Backup platform
- Vendor management records

---

## Audit Period

The audit reviews controls and evidence from a simulated 12-month operating period.

---

## Audit Criteria

Controls are evaluated against:

- Internal policies and procedures
- Security control objectives
- Risk-management expectations
- Access-control practices
- Change-management requirements
- Incident-response procedures
- Vulnerability-management processes
- Third-party risk processes
- Audit-evidence requirements

---

## Audit Approach

The audit uses the following procedures:

1. Review applicable policies and procedures
2. Identify key controls
3. Request supporting evidence
4. Conduct simulated walkthroughs
5. Inspect control documentation
6. Select samples for testing
7. Compare evidence to control requirements
8. Identify control exceptions
9. Assess risk
10. Document findings
11. Obtain management responses
12. Track corrective actions
13. Validate remediation evidence
14. Document closure decisions

---

## Control Areas

### 1. User Provisioning

**Objective:**  
Ensure new user access is authorized and appropriate for job responsibilities.

**Evidence:**

- Access request tickets
- Manager approvals
- Account creation records
- Role assignments

---

### 2. User Termination

**Objective:**  
Ensure access is removed promptly when employees leave the organization.

**Evidence:**

- Termination records
- Disablement logs
- HR notifications
- Access-removal tickets

---

### 3. Privileged Access

**Objective:**  
Ensure elevated access is limited, justified, approved, and periodically reviewed.

**Evidence:**

- Privileged account inventory
- Business justification
- Access review records
- Reviewer approvals

---

### 4. Multi-Factor Authentication

**Objective:**  
Ensure applicable users and privileged accounts are protected with MFA.

**Evidence:**

- MFA enrollment reports
- Configuration screenshots
- Authentication policies
- Exception approvals

---

### 5. Access Reviews

**Objective:**  
Ensure user access is periodically reviewed and unnecessary access is removed.

**Evidence:**

- User access reports
- Quarterly review records
- Manager certification
- Removal evidence

---

### 6. Change Management

**Objective:**  
Ensure production changes are authorized, tested, documented, and approved.

**Evidence:**

- Change tickets
- Test results
- Approval records
- Implementation records
- Emergency-change documentation

---

### 7. Audit Logging

**Objective:**  
Ensure security-relevant events are logged, reviewed, and investigated.

**Evidence:**

- SIEM reports
- Log source inventory
- Review records
- Investigation tickets
- Escalation documentation

---

### 8. Vulnerability Management

**Objective:**  
Ensure vulnerabilities are identified, prioritized, remediated, and tracked.

**Evidence:**

- Vulnerability scan reports
- Remediation tickets
- Aging reports
- Risk-acceptance records
- Patch evidence

---

### 9. Incident Response

**Objective:**  
Ensure security incidents are identified, documented, escalated, contained, and reviewed.

**Evidence:**

- Incident tickets
- Incident response plan
- Investigation records
- Tabletop exercise reports
- Lessons learned

---

### 10. Backup and Recovery

**Objective:**  
Ensure critical data is backed up and recovery capability is tested.

**Evidence:**

- Backup reports
- Restore-test records
- Recovery documentation
- Failed-backup tickets

---

### 11. Third-Party Risk Management

**Objective:**  
Ensure high-risk vendors are assessed before onboarding and periodically reassessed.

**Evidence:**

- Vendor security questionnaires
- Risk assessments
- Contracts
- Compliance reports
- Reassessment records
- Remediation tracking

---

## Sampling Approach

The simulated audit uses judgmental sampling based on:

- Control risk
- Transaction volume
- Privileged access
- System criticality
- Evidence availability
- Prior exceptions

Example samples include:

- 10 user provisioning events
- 10 terminated-user accounts
- 8 privileged accounts
- 10 production changes
- 10 high-risk vulnerabilities
- 5 security incidents
- 5 high-risk vendors
- 4 backup restoration tests

---

## Control Effectiveness Ratings

### Effective

The control is appropriately designed and evidence supports consistent operation.

### Effective with Minor Exception

The control generally operates as intended, but a limited exception was identified.

### Partially Effective

The control exists but does not consistently operate as intended.

### Ineffective

The control is missing or significant deficiencies prevent it from meeting its objective.

### Not Tested

Insufficient evidence was available to complete testing.

---

## Risk Assessment

Audit exceptions are rated using:

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

### Risk Levels

- Low: 1–4
- Medium: 5–10
- High: 11–15
- Critical: 16–25

## Out of Scope

The following activities are excluded from this simulated audit:

- Financial statement auditing
- Penetration testing
- Source-code review
- Legal compliance opinions
- Production-system access
- Real customer data
- Real employee data
