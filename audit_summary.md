# Internal Audit Summary

## Executive Summary

This simulated internal audit evaluated key security and compliance controls at Northbridge Technologies.

The review focused on access management, privileged access, MFA, change management, audit logging, vulnerability management, incident response, backup and recovery, risk management, third-party risk, policy governance, and remediation tracking.

A total of 15 control areas were tested.

The audit identified several control weaknesses requiring corrective action, including high-risk issues involving:

- User termination
- Privileged access
- Multi-factor authentication
- Access review follow-up
- Vulnerability remediation
- Incident response testing
- Third-party reassessment
- Audit finding closure validation

## High-Risk Findings

### 1. User Termination

Two terminated users retained access beyond the expected disablement timeframe.

**Risk:** Unauthorized access by former employees.

**Recommendation:** Automate termination notifications and monitor access removal.

---

### 2. Privileged Access

Three of eight privileged accounts lacked current business justification or recent review evidence.

**Risk:** Excessive privileges may remain active.

**Recommendation:** Perform quarterly privileged-access reviews and retain approval evidence.

---

### 3. Multi-Factor Authentication

Two privileged accounts lacked MFA and did not have approved exceptions.

**Risk:** Compromised credentials may provide unauthorized privileged access.

**Recommendation:** Enforce MFA for all applicable privileged accounts.

---

### 4. Access Reviews

Two sampled access-review findings lacked evidence showing unnecessary access had been removed.

**Risk:** Excessive or inappropriate access may persist.

**Recommendation:** Require remediation evidence before access-review findings are closed.

---

### 5. Vulnerability Management

Three high-risk vulnerabilities exceeded remediation timelines without documented risk acceptance.

**Risk:** Known vulnerabilities may remain exploitable.

**Recommendation:** Enforce severity-based remediation SLAs and formal exception approval.

---

### 6. Incident Response Testing

No current tabletop exercise was available.

**Risk:** Incident response procedures may not operate effectively during a real event.

**Recommendation:** Conduct annual tabletop exercises and track corrective actions.

---

### 7. Third-Party Risk

Two high-risk vendors had outdated reassessment evidence.

**Risk:** Changes in vendor security posture may remain unidentified.

**Recommendation:** Establish recurring reassessment requirements for high-risk vendors.

---

### 8. Remediation Tracking

Two prior audit findings were marked closed without sufficient validation evidence.

**Risk:** Control weaknesses may remain unresolved even though they appear closed.

**Recommendation:** Require independent validation evidence and formal approval before closure.

## Remediation Validation

Audit findings should not be closed solely because management states remediation is complete.

Closure should require:

1. Corrective action completed
2. Supporting evidence provided
3. Evidence reviewed
4. Control retested when necessary
5. Residual risk evaluated
6. Closure approved

---

## Key Audit Themes

The assessment identified several recurring themes:

### Documentation

Some controls existed but lacked sufficient documentation to demonstrate consistent operation.

### Recurring Review

Access, vendor, and security review activities require stronger recurring oversight.

### Evidence Retention

Control owners should retain enough evidence to support future audit and compliance reviews.

### Remediation Governance

Corrective actions require clear owners, deadlines, validation evidence, and closure approval.

### Control Effectiveness

A control should not be considered effective simply because a negative event has not occurred.

The control should be supported by evidence demonstrating that it operates as designed.

## Final Conclusion

The simulated audit identified meaningful opportunities to improve control consistency, evidence retention, recurring review processes, and remediation governance.

The highest-priority issues relate to identity and access management, vulnerability remediation, incident response testing, third-party risk, and audit finding closure.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end internal audit workflow from planning and evidence collection through testing, findings, management response, remediation, and closure validation.

This is a simulated portfolio project and does not represent an audit performed for a real organization.
